Mortgage Underwriter 101 — Summary

Everything essential from the full module, condensed to a 10-minute read.

⚡ In 60 Seconds

Underwriting is the art of deciding whether a loan is an acceptable risk. You evaluate the Four C's — Credit, Capacity, Capital, Collateral — against program guidelines, then issue a decision: approval, suspense, denial, or clear-to-close. Everything flows through two numbers: LTV (loan vs. property value) and DTI (debt vs. income). The AUS (DU or LPA) provides the starting point, but you are the manual underwriter who verifies data integrity, recalculates income, sources assets, and confirms the property. If the numbers don't support the loan, you don't sign it — your name is on the file.

⭐ The 10 Things You Must Remember

  1. You own the file. Never accept someone else's income worksheet, credit analysis, or approval without verifying it yourself. Your name is on the credit decision.
  2. Garbage in, garbage out. If you input wrong data into DU or LPA, you get wrong findings. Data integrity is the foundation of every credit decision.
  3. PITIA is the housing payment. Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance (homeowners/flood/mortgage if required), Association dues.
  4. Total DTI is the ratio that matters. (PITIA + all other debts) ÷ monthly qualifying income. This is what guideline qualification is based on.
  5. Mid-FICO rule: 3 scores → take the middle. 2 scores → take the lower. Two borrowers → use the lower of the two mid-scores.
  6. Purchase vs. refinance: Purchase uses the lower of appraised value or purchase price. Refinance always uses the current appraised value.
  7. AUS is a starting point, not the final word. DU and LPA produce the bare minimum conditions. Investor overlays and your own due diligence always apply on top.
  8. Income has categories, not one formula. Wage earner, self-employed, rental, and fixed income each require their own calculation method.
  9. Assets must be sourced. Cash sitting in a safe isn't acceptable. Large deposits need explanation + proof. Gifts need a letter + transfer evidence + donor ability.
  10. If you can't support it, don't sign it. FHA/VA authority is your name on the file. If the numbers don't work, escalate — don't approve hoping it works out.

📐 Cheatsheet — Critical Formulas

LTV (Loan-to-Value) 1st Mortgage Loan Amount ÷ Lower(Appraised Value, Purchase Price)
CLTV (Combined LTV) (1st Loan + Subordinate Balance) ÷ Lower(Appraised Value, Purchase Price)
HCLTV (High Combined LTV) (1st Loan + Subordinate Max Limit) ÷ Lower(Appraised Value, Purchase Price)
Housing Ratio (Front-End DTI) PITIA ÷ Total Monthly Qualifying Income
Total DTI (Back-End) (PITIA + All Other Debts) ÷ Total Monthly Qualifying Income
Hourly Wage Earner (Hourly Rate × Weekly Hours × 52) ÷ 12
Varying Hours (YTD Average) (YTD Earnings + Prior-Year Earnings) ÷ (Months YTD + 12)
Bi-Weekly Pay (Gross Pay × 26) ÷ 12
Semi-Monthly Pay (Gross Pay × 24) ÷ 12
Rental Income (New Purchase, No Tax Return) Market Rent × 75%

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • ⚠️ Accepting someone else's income worksheet without recalculating — you own the decision.
  • ⚠️ Trusting DU/LPA findings without verifying that the data entered matches the documents.
  • ⚠️ Missing after-tax deductions on a pay stub (child support garnishment, company loan repayment) that must be added to DTI.
  • ⚠️ Using the highest of three FICO scores instead of the middle — or using the higher of two mid-scores across borrowers.
  • ⚠️ Excluding an auto lease payment because it "looks like" an auto loan on the credit report. Leases cannot be excluded.
  • ⚠️ Using an old bank statement. Verify the most recent statement — a 2-year-old statement is expired.
  • ⚠️ Accepting a large deposit without sourcing it. If it can't be sourced, back it out of the balance.
  • ⚠️ Accepting a gift without a fully executed gift letter, proof of transfer, and donor ability documentation.
  • ⚠️ Forgetting to add the child support on the credit report to liabilities — a common reason AUS findings understate the DTI.
  • ⚠️ Overlooking declining market trends on the appraisal — a material issue that can affect LTV and program eligibility.
  • ⚠️ Signing an approval on an FHA/VA loan when the file doesn't support the decision. Seek a second opinion or escalate.
  • ⚠️ Missing repairs noted in the purchase contract — health and safety repairs require proof of completion before final approval.
