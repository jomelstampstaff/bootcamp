FHA/VA Processing — Summary

Everything essential from the full module, condensed to a 10-minute read.

⚡ In 60 Seconds

FHA and VA are government-backed loan programs that let borrowers qualify with lower down payments, more flexible credit, and — for VA — zero down. In exchange for the higher risk, the government charges the borrower an insurance premium (FHA: MIP) or a one-time guarantee fee (VA: funding fee). Both programs come with special rules you won't see on conventional: FHA has Minimum Property Requirements (MPRs), VA has Minimum Property Requirements (MPRs) too, and both require their own appraisals (FHA: form 1004 with MPR checklist; VA: Notice of Value / NOV via a VA-assigned appraiser). Underwriting authority is restricted — you need your FHA DE (CHUMS ID) or your VA SAR to underwrite these loans.

⭐ The 10 Things You Must Remember

  1. FHA = insurance. VA = guarantee. The government backs the loan, letting lenders offer lower down payments and more flexible credit in exchange for the borrower paying MIP (FHA) or a funding fee (VA).
  2. FHA MIP is two parts: Upfront MIP (typically 1.75% of the base loan, financed) and Annual MIP (monthly, added to PITIA). On most FHA loans, MIP lasts the life of the loan unless the borrower puts 10% or more down.
  3. VA Funding Fee is one-time. Typically financed into the loan. Some veterans are exempt (service-connected disabilities); others pay a percentage based on down payment, first use vs. subsequent use, and loan type.
  4. VA entitlement drives the down payment. Full entitlement = zero down. Partial entitlement may require a down payment to cover the gap between the loan amount and the guaranty.
  5. You need the right authority. FHA loans require a DE (CHUMS ID). VA loans require SAR certification. Without them, you cannot underwrite the loan.
  6. MPRs apply on both programs. Health & safety issues — peeling paint (pre-1978), exposed wiring, roof deficiencies, structural issues — must be repaired before closing. The appraiser flags them; you condition for proof.
  7. FHA Amendatory Clause and VA Escape Clause protect the borrower if the appraisal comes in below the sales price. Both must be present in the purchase contract; without them, the contract is non-compliant.
  8. FHA case numbers and VA NOVs are tied to the property and borrower. Case numbers expire. If the appraisal or case number has expired, you must order a new one.
  9. Termite inspections may be required. VA often requires them (state-dependent). FHA may require them based on the state or the appraiser's findings.
  10. Streamline refinances have reduced documentation but are not no-doc. IRRRL (VA) and FHA Streamline still require a benefit to the borrower and verification of the existing loan.

📐 Cheatsheet — Critical Figures & Formulas

FHA Upfront MIP Base Loan Amount × 1.75% (typically financed into the loan)
FHA Annual MIP Base Loan Amount × Annual MIP Rate ÷ 12 = Monthly MIP (added to PITIA)
FHA Minimum Down Payment 3.5% (with 580+ FICO) | 10% (with 500–579 FICO)
VA Full Entitlement Zero down payment, no VA loan limit (as of 2020)
VA Funding Fee (First Use, Zero Down) 2.15% (varies by down payment, first use vs. subsequent, and loan type)
PITIA with FHA MIP P + I + T + I (homeowners) + Monthly MIP + HOA (if applicable)
FHA DTI Limits Up to 43% (AUS Approve/Eligible) | Up to 50% with documented compensating factors
FHA MIP Example:
Base loan: $300,000
Upfront MIP: $300,000 × 1.75% = $5,250 (financed)
Annual MIP rate: 0.85% → $300,000 × 0.85% = $2,550/year
Monthly MIP: $2,550 ÷ 12
= $212.50/month added to PITIA

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • ⚠️ Forgetting to add monthly FHA MIP to PITIA — the DTI will be wrong if you omit it.
  • ⚠️ Assuming all FHA MIP drops off at 78% LTV — that rule applies to conventional PMI, not FHA (with exceptions for 10%+ down).
  • ⚠️ Missing the FHA Amendatory Clause or the VA Escape Clause in the purchase contract.
  • ⚠️ Failing to verify VA entitlement — using a full-entitlement assumption on a partial-entitlement borrower triggers a surprise down payment.
  • ⚠️ Overlooking MPR-related repairs noted in the appraisal — peeling paint, exposed wiring, roof issues must be cleared before closing.
  • ⚠️ Ordering a VA appraisal without confirming the case number / NOV is properly assigned and current.
  • ⚠️ Letting an FHA case number expire — you'll need a new one and a new appraisal.
  • ⚠️ Forgetting the termite inspection requirement on VA loans in states where it applies.
  • ⚠️ Using the wrong DTI thresholds — FHA has AUS-based limits and manual limits that differ.
  • ⚠️ Underwriting an FHA or VA loan without the proper certification (DE / CHUMS or SAR).
  • ⚠️ Missing the VA funding fee exemption for eligible disabled veterans.
  • ⚠️ Treating an IRRRL or FHA Streamline as "no-doc" — still requires verification of existing loan and borrower benefit.
